# Sermon Studio — user guide

## What it does

Write and organise sermon outlines on your phone, with the Bible a tap away.
Everything stays on your device.

## Your first outline

1. Tap **+** on the Sermon Library screen.
2. Give it a title.
3. Start writing in the Outline box.

Your work saves automatically about a second and a half after you stop typing.
There is no Save button.

## Scripture references

As you type, the app recognises references and shows them as chips under the
title. It understands:

- `John 3:16` and `Jn 3:16`
- `Matthew 5:1-12` and `Matt 5:1-12`
- `Psalm 23` (whole chapter)
- `Romans 8:28-30`
- `Genesis 1:1, 3` (two separate verses)
- `Matthew 5:1-6:4` (across chapters)
- Tagalog and Cebuano book names, e.g. `Mga Awit 23`, `Juan 3:16`

Tap a chip to open the passage.

### The passage popup

- Scroll for longer passages
- **Copy** — passage plus reference, to the clipboard
- **Insert** — adds the passage to the end of your outline
- **Chapter** — opens the full chapter in the reader
- **Zoom** — larger or smaller text, for reading from the pulpit

## Reading

Tap the book icon on the Library screen. If you set a second translation in
Settings, each verse shows both, lined up.

## Searching

The search screen does two things:

- Type words (`shepherd`, `love enemies`) to find verses containing them.
- Type a reference (`John 3:16`) to jump straight to it.

It matches words, not meaning. A question like "what was Jesus' first miracle"
will not work — search for distinctive words from the passage instead.

## Settings

**Appearance** — light, dark, or follow the system. On Android 12 and later the
app can match your wallpaper colours.

**Bible translations** — shows which are installed. Tap an installed one to make
it your primary. No Bible text is included until a module is added; until then
the reader will say the module is not installed.

**Backup** — automatic local backup of your outlines.

## Your data

Outlines, notes and bookmarks are stored on your device and are not sent
anywhere. The app works with no network connection.

The exception, if you ever configure one: a licensed translation reached
through its publisher's API sends the reference you are looking up to that
publisher. Public-domain translations are fully offline.

## Not available yet

- Bold, italic, headings and colours in the editor
- PDF and Word export and import
- AI outline generation
- Meaning-based search
